## 01 — The Beginning

<div align="center">

### 🗻 Prompt 1

**"Make me a photo of Japan."**

</div>

A simple prompt used to generate an image inspired by Japan's beautiful scenery, featuring Mount Fuji, cherry blossoms, a traditional pagoda, and a sunset.

---

## 02 — Adding Details

<div align="center">

### 🎆 Prompt 2

**"A girl wearing a black and white kimono at a Sakura Festival, enjoying takoyaki with Mount Fuji in the background. Also include colorful fireworks, food stalls offering a variety of Japanese dishes, glowing lanterns, cherry blossoms, and many people dressed in different colored kimonos. The main focus is on the girl."**

</div>

I used ChatGPT to improve my first prompt by adding more specific descriptions, surroundings, cultural elements, and visual details to make the generated image more interesting and meaningful.

---

## 03 — What Changed?

| Prompt 1 | Prompt 2 |
|:--|:--|
| Simple description | Detailed description |
| General scenery | Sakura Festival |
| Few visual details | Multiple visual elements |
| Basic concept | Clear main subject |

---

## 04 — Reflection

The first image presents the beautiful scenery of Japan, while the second creates a more detailed, vibrant, and lively Sakura Festival scene, with the girl as the main subject.

Through this activity, I learned that AI image generation depends greatly on how instructions are written. Adding details about the subject, setting, colors, lighting, and atmosphere can make the output more creative and closer to the idea I imagined.
