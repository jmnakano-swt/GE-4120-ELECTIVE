# EXPLORING AND IMPROVING AI PROMPTS

*Think clearly. Prompt specifically. Get better results.*

An exploration of how the quality and clarity of instructions influence the responses generated by Artificial Intelligence.

<br/>

</div>

---

## 01. Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become a useful tool for learning, research, writing, and everyday tasks. However, getting accurate and relevant results does not depend only on the AI tool being used. It also depends on how the user communicates instructions to it.

This activity focuses on exploring and improving AI prompts by learning how to provide clear, specific, and detailed instructions.

The main idea is simple: **The more specific the prompt, the better the AI can understand what the user needs.**

---

## 02. What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt engineering is the process of creating and improving instructions given to an AI system to produce more useful, relevant, and accurate responses.

A prompt serves as the instruction or question that guides the AI in generating an answer.

A good prompt should communicate:

- **Clarity** – Make the instruction easy to understand.
- **Specificity** – Include the exact information needed.
- **Context** – Provide background information about the task.
- **Format** – Explain how the answer should be presented.
- **Purpose** – Identify the expected outcome.

---

## 03. Why Being Specific Matters

AI systems generate responses based on the instructions and context provided by users.

When a prompt is too general, the AI may produce answers that are vague, incomplete, or different from what the user expects.

When a prompt is specific, it has more guidance about the topic, audience, tone, and desired output.

| VAGUE PROMPT | SPECIFIC PROMPT |
|---|---|
| Explain nursing. | Explain the role of a registered nurse in patient care using simple English. |
| Make a summary. | Summarize this lesson into five bullet points using student-friendly language. |
| Create a quiz. | Create 10 multiple-choice questions about infection prevention with answers and rationales. |
| Write an essay. | Write a three-paragraph reflection about the importance of technology in nursing education. |

**Key Lesson:** Giving AI more relevant details helps it generate responses that are better aligned with the user's expectations.

---

## 04. The Elements of a Good Prompt

A well-structured prompt can contain the following elements:

| ELEMENT | PURPOSE | EXAMPLE |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Defines the AI's perspective. | Act as a nursing instructor. |
| Context | Provides background information. | I am a fourth-year nursing student. |
| Task | Explains what needs to be done. | Explain the pathophysiology of pneumonia. |
| Specificity | Sets clear requirements. | Include the causes and major clinical manifestations. |
| Format | Organizes the output. | Use bullet points and simple explanations. |
| Tone | Establishes the writing style. | Use easy English suitable for students. |

## 05. Exploring and Improving Prompts

One of the main lessons of this activity is understanding the difference between a general prompt and an improved prompt.

### Example 1: Educational Prompt

**Initial Prompt:**
> Explain heart failure.

**Improved Prompt:**
> Explain heart failure in simple English for a nursing student. Include its definition, causes, pathophysiology, signs and symptoms, and nursing management. Organize the explanation using headings and bullet points.

**Improvement:** The revised prompt identifies the audience, required topics, language, and format.

### Example 2: Writing Prompt

**Initial Prompt:**
> Write a reflection.

**Improved Prompt:**
> Write a three-paragraph personal reflection about the importance of digital technology in education. Use first-person perspective, simple English, and a natural student tone.

**Improvement:** The revised prompt provides the topic, length, perspective, and writing style.

### Example 3: Research Prompt

**Initial Prompt:**
> Give me information about hypertension.

**Improved Prompt:**
> Explain hypertension for a nursing research presentation. Include its definition, risk factors, common manifestations, complications, and prevention. Use reliable medical references published from 2022 onward and provide citations.

**Improvement:** The revised prompt specifies the purpose, scope, and source requirements.

## 06. The Benefits of Effective Prompting

- **More Relevant Answers** – Helps AI understand the user's actual needs.
- **Better Accuracy** – Provides important context that can reduce misunderstandings.
- **Time Efficiency** – Reduces repeated instructions and unnecessary revisions.
- **Organized Results** – Allows users to request specific formats and structures.
- **Improved Learning** – Helps students explore topics and understand difficult concepts.

### Common Prompting Mistakes

| MISTAKE | HOW TO IMPROVE IT |
|---|---|
| Asking very broad questions | Narrow down the topic. |
| Providing little context | Explain the situation or purpose. |
| Not specifying the format | State whether you want a table, list, or paragraph. |
| Giving unclear instructions | Use direct and understandable language. |
| Accepting every AI response | Verify information using reliable sources. |

---

## 07. Application in Nursing Education

AI prompting can be useful in different nursing academic activities when used responsibly.

**1. Studying and Reviewing**

Students can request simplified explanations of diseases, medications, and nursing concepts.

**2. Case Study Analysis**

AI can help organize information and explain nursing concepts related to patient scenarios without exposing confidential patient information.

**3. Creating Practice Questions**

Students can generate practice quizzes, multiple-choice questions, and rationales for examination preparation.

**4. Writing Assistance**

AI can help improve grammar, organize ideas, and develop clearer academic explanations.

**5. Research Support**

Students can use AI to explore research topics, identify key concepts, and organize preliminary information while verifying sources independently.

---

## 08. Responsible Use of AI

Although AI can be helpful, its responses should not automatically be considered correct.

Users should remember to:

- Verify important information using reliable sources.
- Avoid sharing confidential or personal information.
- Review AI-generated content before submitting academic work.
- Use AI as a learning assistant, not a replacement for critical thinking.
- Follow school guidelines regarding AI-assisted work.
- Recognize that AI may generate inaccurate or misleading information.

> **Remember:** A well-written prompt can improve the quality of an AI response, but it does not guarantee that every answer will be accurate. Always evaluate and verify the information.
