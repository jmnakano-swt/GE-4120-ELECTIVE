## 01 — Introduction

For this activity, I explored how Artificial Intelligence can help create a professional resume using two AI tools: **ChatGPT** and **Claude AI**.

The goal was to understand how each tool generates resume content, organizes information, and presents a person's qualifications and experiences.

## 02 — AI Tools Used

### 𖤐 ChatGPT

ChatGPT is an AI tool developed by OpenAI that can assist users in writing, organizing, and improving resume content.

**What I explored:**
- Generating a professional resume.
- Improving sentence structure and grammar.
- Organizing educational background and skills.
- Creating clear and professional descriptions.

### 𖤐 Claude AI

Claude is an AI assistant developed by Anthropic that can also help users create and improve professional documents, including resumes.

**What I explored:**
- Generating resume content from provided information.
- Improving the wording of personal experiences.
- Organizing qualifications and achievements.
- Creating polished and readable descriptions.

---

## 03 — Resume Generation Process

### Step 1: Prepare Personal Information

I prepared the necessary information for my resume, including:

- Personal and contact information
- Educational background
- Work experience
- Skills and qualifications
- Achievements and certifications

### Step 2: Generate Using ChatGPT

I provided my information to ChatGPT and asked it to generate a professional resume with organized sections and clear descriptions.

### Step 3: Generate Using Claude AI

I used the same information and instructions in Claude AI to generate another version of my resume.

### Step 4: Compare the Results

After generating both resumes, I examined their formatting, writing style, organization, and overall presentation.

---

## 04 — ChatGPT vs. Claude AI

| Comparison | ChatGPT | Claude AI |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Writing Style | Clear and adaptable | Natural and polished |
| Organization | Structured sections | Well-developed flow |
| Content | Professional descriptions | Detailed explanations |
| Editing | Helps refine and rewrite | Helps improve clarity and tone |
| Overall | Versatile writing assistant | Strong writing and document assistant |

*Note: These are general observations. Actual results may vary depending on the prompts and information provided.*

---

## 05 — Key Differences in AI

Although ChatGPT and Claude AI can perform similar tasks, they may produce different results depending on how they interpret instructions.

ChatGPT provided a flexible approach to organizing and improving resume content, while Claude AI offered another writing style and presentation of the same information.

Both tools can save time and help users create professional documents, but their outputs should still be reviewed and personalized to ensure accuracy.

---

## 06 — Personal Reflection

Through this activity, I learned that AI tools can make resume creation easier and more convenient. Comparing ChatGPT and Claude AI helped me understand that different AI systems can generate different writing styles even when given the same information.

I also realized that AI should be used as an assistant rather than a replacement for personal judgment. A resume should accurately represent an individual's real experiences, skills, and qualifications.
