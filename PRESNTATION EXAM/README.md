# GOOGLE FORMS & GOOGLE MEET

*Create. Collect. Connect. Collaborate.*

A guide to understanding two essential digital tools used for information collection and virtual communication.

<br/>

</div>

---

## 01. Introduction

Technology has become an important part of education, communication, and everyday activities. Google Forms and Google Meet are two applications under Google Workspace that help people work more efficiently.

- **Google Forms** is used to create surveys, quizzes, questionnaires, and registration forms.
- **Google Meet** is used for video conferences, online classes, meetings, and virtual discussions.

Together, these applications make digital learning and collaboration more accessible.

---

## 02. Google Forms

<div align="center">
<img src="https://img.icons8.com/color/96/google-forms.png" width="75"/>

### Information Collection Tool

*Where questions become information.*
</div>

### What is Google Forms?

Google Forms is an online application developed by Google that allows users to create digital forms, surveys, questionnaires, and quizzes. It automatically collects and organizes submitted responses.

### How Does It Work?

| STEP | PROCESS | DESCRIPTION |
|:---:|---|---|
| 01 | Create | Start with a blank form or template. |
| 02 | Add Questions | Choose question types and enter questions. |
| 03 | Share | Distribute the form through a link or email. |
| 04 | Collect | Receive submitted responses online. |
| 05 | Analyze | Review results and organize data using Google Sheets. |

### Main Features

- **Question Types** – Multiple choice, checkboxes, dropdowns, and short answers.
- **Customization** – Personalize themes, colors, and headers.
- **Quiz Mode** – Create assessments with answer keys and scoring.
- **Response Summary** – View automatically generated charts and results.
- **Google Sheets Integration** – Organize and analyze collected information.

### Uses of Google Forms

| AREA | EXAMPLE |
|---|---|
| Education | Online quizzes and assessments |
| Research | Surveys and questionnaires |
| Events | Registration and attendance |
| Business | Customer feedback |
| Healthcare Education | Nursing knowledge assessments |

### Advantages and Limitations

**Advantages**
- Easy to create and share.
- Automatically collects responses.
- Reduces paper usage.
- Supports surveys and quizzes.
- Integrates with Google Sheets.

**Limitations**
- Requires internet access for online use.
- Limited advanced customization.
- Not designed for complex data analysis.
- Response accuracy depends on participants.
- Some features depend on account settings.

### Nursing Student Application

A nursing instructor can create a Google Forms quiz about infection prevention. Students can answer online, while the instructor reviews the results to identify topics that need further discussion.

## 03. Google Meet

<div align="center">
<img src="https://img.icons8.com/color/96/google-meet.png" width="75"/>

### Video Communication Tool

*Bringing people closer, wherever they are.*
</div>

### What is Google Meet?

Google Meet is a video communication platform developed by Google. It allows users to communicate through online video meetings and audio calls.

It is commonly used for virtual classes, business meetings, interviews, consultations, and group discussions.

### How Does It Work?

| STEP | PROCESS | DESCRIPTION |
|:---:|---|---|
| 01 | Create or Schedule | Start an instant meeting or schedule through Google Calendar. |
| 02 | Share Link | Invite participants using a meeting link. |
| 03 | Join | Open the link and check camera and microphone settings. |
| 04 | Communicate | Talk, send messages, and share screens. |
| 05 | End Meeting | Leave the meeting when the discussion is finished. |

### Main Features

- **Video Conferencing** – Communicate with participants in real time.
- **Screen Sharing** – Present slides, documents, and other materials.
- **In-Meeting Chat** – Send written messages during meetings.
- **Live Captions** – Display spoken words as text when supported.
- **Calendar Integration** – Schedule and organize virtual meetings.
- **Meeting Recording** – Record meetings on eligible accounts and plans.

### Uses of Google Meet

| AREA | EXAMPLE |
|---|---|
| Education | Online classes and virtual lectures |
| Business | Team meetings and project discussions |
| Healthcare Education | Case presentations and consultations |
| Interviews | Online job interviews |
| Collaboration | Group projects and planning |

### Advantages and Limitations

**Advantages**
- Supports real-time communication.
- Makes remote learning possible.
- Provides screen-sharing capabilities.
- Allows easy meeting invitations.
- Integrates with Google Calendar.

**Limitations**
- Requires a stable internet connection.
- Audio and video delays may occur.
- Extended screen time may cause fatigue.
- Requires compatible devices and permissions.
- Some advanced features require eligible plans.

### Nursing Student Application

Nursing students can use Google Meet to discuss patient case studies, conduct group reporting, practice presentations, and attend virtual consultations with instructors.

## 04. Google Forms vs. Google Meet

Although both applications are part of Google Workspace, they serve different purposes.

| CATEGORY | GOOGLE FORMS | GOOGLE MEET |
|---|---|---|
| Main Purpose | Information collection | Virtual communication |
| Primary Function | Creates surveys and quizzes | Hosts online meetings |
| Interaction | Submitted responses | Real-time discussions |
| Output | Collected data and summaries | Communication and collaboration |
| Common Use | Assessments and questionnaires | Classes and presentations |

## 05. Applications in Nursing

Digital applications can support nursing education by making learning activities more organized and accessible.

### Google Forms in Nursing

- Creating quizzes about nursing procedures.
- Collecting feedback after clinical discussions.
- Conducting academic research surveys.
- Registering participants for health education activities.
- Evaluating students' knowledge and understanding.

### Google Meet in Nursing

- Conducting virtual case presentations.
- Attending online lectures and consultations.
- Discussing nursing care plans with classmates.
- Coordinating group academic projects.
- Participating in virtual learning activities.

> **Privacy Reminder:** Patient information and confidential health records must be protected. Do not enter or share identifiable patient information through ordinary forms or meetings without proper authorization and appropriate privacy safeguards.
