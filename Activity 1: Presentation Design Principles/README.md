## ☕ 01. INTRODUCTION
<sub>THE IDEA BEHIND THE DESIGN</sub>

For Activity 1, I applied the core principles of presentation design using **Canva** to recreate and develop a simple but effective social media graphic: a **"Free Coffee" poster**.

The wording and concept were based on the Canva design provided for the activity. Rather than creating the concept or wording from scratch, I followed the given content and focused on applying the presentation design principles discussed in class.

The poster features the main message:

> **FREE COFFEE**

It also includes the provided tagline:

> *"Free coffee. No catch. Just caffeine and good vibes."*

My main focus was to organize and present the existing information using appropriate visual design principles.

---

## 🍂 02. DESIGN CONCEPT
<sub>WARM COLORS · SIMPLE LAYOUT · CLEAR MESSAGE</sub>

The main idea behind this activity is:

> **"Design guides attention before content is read."**

When someone looks at a poster, their attention is usually directed toward certain visual elements before they begin reading all the information.

For this reason, the design emphasizes **"FREE COFFEE"** while keeping the supporting information organized and easy to read.

### 🎨 Color Story

The poster uses a **brown-and-white color scheme** inspired by the warmth and comfort of coffee.

| Color | Purpose |
|:---|:---|
| ☕ Coffee Brown | Emphasizes the coffee theme |
| 🥛 Creamy White | Creates a clean background |
| 🍮 Caramel | Adds warmth and visual interest |
| 🤎 Dark Espresso | Creates contrast and emphasis |

The goal was to keep the poster simple without adding unnecessary elements.

### 01. Contrast ☕

The first principle I applied was **Contrast**.

I made sure that the main text, **"FREE COFFEE,"** stood out prominently against the white background. The strong brown-and-white contrast immediately attracts the viewer's attention to the most important information.

This follows the principle that:

> Key elements should be readable and noticeable from across the room.

The main message is visually stronger than the supporting information, creating a clear visual hierarchy.

**How it improved the design:**
- Makes the main message noticeable.
- Directs the viewer's attention.
- Improves readability.
- Creates a clear visual hierarchy.
- Makes the poster easier to understand at a glance.

### 02. Repetition 🤎

The second principle I applied was **Repetition**.

I maintained consistency by reusing the same font style and brown-and-white color scheme throughout the poster.

The consistent colors and typography make the different elements feel connected rather than appearing as separate or unrelated parts.

Repetition creates consistency and helps the audience learn to read the design quickly.

**How it improved the design:**
- Maintains visual consistency.
- Creates a cohesive appearance.
- Makes the design look organized.
- Strengthens the coffee-related theme.
- Avoids unnecessary visual distractions.

### 03. Alignment 🍮

The third principle I applied was **Alignment**.

I made sure that the different elements of the poster were positioned according to a clear structure. The title, date, location, and booth number follow an organized layout rather than being placed randomly.

Using Canva's alignment and grid tools helped me position the elements consistently.

Alignment creates visual order and makes the content easier for the audience to follow.

**How it improved the design:**
- Creates a clean and organized layout.
- Establishes visual structure.
- Makes information easier to scan.
- Prevents elements from appearing randomly placed.
- Makes the poster look more polished.

### 04. Proximity 🌾

The fourth principle I applied was **Proximity**.

I grouped information that belongs together so that the relationship between the different pieces of information is immediately clear.

For example, the event details, including the date, time, and location, are grouped together.

Meanwhile, the call-to-action:

> **"First come, First served"**

is positioned separately at the bottom.

This allows the audience to distinguish the event information from the call-to-action.

**How it improved the design:**
- Groups related information together.
- Separates different types of information.
- Reduces visual confusion.
- Improves readability.
- Makes important details easier to locate.

## ☕ 04. OVERALL EFFECT OF THE DESIGN

Applying these four principles improved the effectiveness of the poster because each design element has a clear visual purpose.

### Faster Comprehension
The viewer can immediately recognize that the poster is about free coffee because the main message is emphasized through size, contrast, and placement.

### Better Retention
The clean and consistent design makes the main message visually distinct and easier to remember.

### Better Organization
Alignment and proximity make the information easier to navigate. Related details are grouped together, while different sections are separated appropriately.

## 🧸 05. PERSONAL REFLECTION

This activity helped me understand that presentation design is not simply about making a poster look attractive.

It is also about understanding **how people see and process information**.

By applying Contrast, Repetition, Alignment, and Proximity, I learned how small design decisions can affect the way an audience understands a message.

I also learned that a designer does not always need to add more elements to make a design effective. Sometimes, keeping the design simple and organizing the existing elements properly can communicate the message more effectively.

Since the main content and wording were provided in the original design, my focus was on applying the design principles to present these elements clearly and effectively.
