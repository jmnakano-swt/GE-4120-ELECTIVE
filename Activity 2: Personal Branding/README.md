## I. THE BRAND STORY

For Activity 2, I developed a visual identity for a fictional brand called **The Mori's Club**.

The project focused on establishing a cohesive color palette and typography system that reflects the brand's personality, values, and aesthetic direction.

Rather than selecting colors and fonts based solely on appearance, I considered how each visual element could communicate a particular emotion and contribute to a recognizable brand identity.

The final output is a **Color Palette and Typography Set**, serving as the foundation of the brand's visual identity.

> "True elegance is found in the harmony of every detail."

---

## II. CREATIVE DIRECTION

### The Vision

The Mori's Club embraces a visual identity inspired by understated luxury, feminine beauty, and timeless sophistication.

The desired brand personality is:

- Elegant and refined
- Soft and feminine
- Romantic and graceful
- Clean and minimal
- Natural and harmonious
- Sophisticated and timeless

The concept combines the delicate qualities of feminine beauty with the richness of classic luxury.

The overall direction reflects a clean beauty aesthetic, balancing soft pastel shades with deeper, more expressive colors.

### The Visual Language

The identity is built around five carefully selected colors and three complementary typography styles.

Each element has a specific purpose, ensuring that the brand maintains a consistent appearance across different applications.

### 01 — BURGUNDY
`#6E2638` · THE SIGNATURE SHADE

Burgundy is the defining accent of The Mori's Club.

Its deep wine-red appearance communicates confidence, richness, elegance, and sophistication. It gives the softer shades more depth and establishes a recognizable visual presence.

It also introduces a sense of classic luxury, making the brand feel mature and refined.

**Applications:** Brand logo, primary headings, titles, borders, and important highlights.

### 02 — DUSTY ROSE
`#C98F9D` · THE ROMANTIC SHADE

Dusty rose introduces warmth, femininity, and romance.

Its muted appearance creates a graceful and understated aesthetic without appearing overly bright or playful.

It complements burgundy beautifully and softens the overall visual identity.

**Applications:** Supporting backgrounds, decorative elements, secondary headings, and brand accents.

### 03 — BABY PINK
`#F4D6DC` · THE DELICATE SHADE

Baby pink represents softness, delicacy, and youthful femininity.

Its light pastel appearance creates a gentle atmosphere and strengthens the romantic character of the brand.

It provides a beautiful contrast against the deeper burgundy shade.

**Applications:** Backgrounds, subtle highlights, packaging details, and decorative accents.

### 04 — OLIVE GREEN
`#68704A` · THE NATURAL SHADE

Olive green introduces an earthy and organic quality to the palette.

It communicates freshness, nature, and balance, complementing the brand's clean beauty concept.

Its muted tone prevents the color palette from becoming overly sweet or monochromatic.

**Applications:** Botanical details, secondary graphics, small highlights, and natural-themed elements.

### 05 — IVORY
`#FFF9EF` · THE FOUNDATION SHADE

Ivory serves as the primary neutral color of the identity.

Its warm appearance communicates simplicity, cleanliness, and understated elegance.

Unlike pure white, ivory creates a softer and more inviting atmosphere while allowing the other colors to stand out.

**Applications:** Main backgrounds, negative space, layouts, and supporting design elements.

---

## IV. THE ART OF COLOR HARMONY

The five colors work together to establish a distinctive visual language.

| Color | Emotional Association |
|:---|:---|
| Burgundy | Luxury, depth, confidence |
| Dusty Rose | Romance, warmth, femininity |
| Baby Pink | Delicacy, softness, grace |
| Olive Green | Nature, freshness, balance |
| Ivory | Simplicity, warmth, cleanliness |

Burgundy serves as the visual anchor, while dusty rose and baby pink introduce softness.

Olive green provides a natural contrast, and ivory balances the entire composition.

Together, these shades create an identity that is feminine without being overly delicate, and luxurious without appearing excessive.

## V. THE TYPOGRAPHY COLLECTION

<div align="center">

<sub>THREE TYPEFACES · THREE DISTINCT ROLES</sub>

# The Art of Typography

*Where elegance meets readability.*

</div>

### 01 — DISPLAY TYPOGRAPHY

**Noto Serif Display Extra Condensed**

<blockquote>
  <div align="center">

  <title size="2xl" color="#6E2638" weight="medium">The Mori's Club</title>

  <text color="#A66B78" size="xs">TIMELESS · REFINED · EDITORIAL</text>

  </div>
</blockquote>

The display font establishes the brand's visual personality.

Its serif details create a classic and sophisticated appearance, while its extra-condensed structure introduces a modern editorial quality.

The combination reflects the balance between traditional elegance and contemporary design.

**Primary applications:**
- Brand name and logo
- Main headings
- Editorial titles
- Brand statements
- Large visual elements

### 02 — BODY TYPOGRAPHY

**Garet**

<blockquote>
  <div align="center">

  <title size="xl" color="#68704A" weight="medium">Beauty in simplicity.</title>

  <text color="#725E5E" size="sm">A modern approach to visual communication through clean, balanced, and readable typography.</text>

  </div>
</blockquote>

Garet provides the clean and modern foundation of the typography system.

Its legibility makes it suitable for longer descriptions and supporting information.

It complements the serif display font by creating a visual contrast that keeps the brand sophisticated without sacrificing functionality.

**Primary applications:**
- Descriptions
- Paragraphs
- Captions
- Supporting information
- General brand communication

### 03 — ACCENT TYPOGRAPHY

**Script-like Accent Font**

<blockquote>
  <div align="center">

  <title size="2xl" color="#C98F9D" italic weight="medium">with love, always</title>

  <text color="#A66B78" size="xs">PERSONALITY · GRACE · EXPRESSION</text>

  </div>
</blockquote>

The accent typeface adds a romantic and personal quality to the brand.

Its script-like appearance creates visual interest and introduces a softer element to complement the structured serif and sans-serif fonts.

It is intended for selective use to avoid overwhelming the overall typography system.

**Primary applications:**
- Short phrases
- Decorative labels
- Small highlights
- Special emphasis
- Brand details

## VI. THE TYPOGRAPHIC HIERARCHY

The three fonts work together through a defined hierarchy.

| Font | Purpose | Character |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Noto Serif Display Extra Condensed | Display | Classic elegance |
| Garet | Body | Modern simplicity |
| Script-like Accent Font | Accent | Romantic expression |

This combination allows the brand to maintain visual variety while ensuring that each typeface has a clear and functional purpose.

## VII. THE COMPLETE BRAND EXPERIENCE

The color palette and typography system establish a unified visual identity for The Mori's Club.

The colors communicate the emotional atmosphere, while the typography defines the brand's visual voice.

The result is a balance of:

**FEMININITY** — Expressed through soft pink tones and graceful typography.

**SOPHISTICATION** — Established through rich burgundy and elegant serif details.

**NATURAL BEAUTY** — Introduced through muted olive green.

**SIMPLICITY** — Maintained through ivory backgrounds and clean typography.

**ROMANCE** — Reflected through dusty rose and delicate accent elements.

Each visual decision contributes to the same overall brand personality.

## VIII. BRAND CONSISTENCY & APPLICATION

A recognizable brand identity requires consistency across different platforms and materials.

The established visual system can be applied to:

| Application | Brand Implementation |
|:---|:---|
| Social Media | Editorial posts and promotional graphics |
| Product Packaging | Elegant labels and packaging |
| Business Cards | Minimalist brand presentation |
| Advertisements | Consistent promotional materials |
| Website | Cohesive digital identity |
| Presentations | Unified visual communication |

Maintaining the same colors, typography, and design direction allows the brand to remain recognizable regardless of where it appears.

---

## IX. PERSONAL REFLECTION

This activity helped me understand that creating a brand identity involves more than simply choosing colors and fonts that look attractive.

Every design choice communicates something about the brand.

The colors I selected were intended to express softness, femininity, romance, nature, cleanliness, elegance, and sophistication.

Burgundy gave the palette depth and a distinctive accent, while the softer pink shades maintained its romantic character. Olive green introduced a natural element, and ivory provided balance.

I also learned the importance of assigning specific roles to different typefaces. A display font can establish personality, while a body font must prioritize readability. An accent font can introduce creativity when used appropriately.

Most importantly, I realized that a successful visual identity requires a balance between **aesthetic appeal, consistency, and functionality**.

This activity encouraged me to become more intentional with my design decisions and understand how individual elements can work together to create a complete and recognizable brand.

---

## X. KEY TAKEAWAY

This activity demonstrated how color and typography can communicate a brand's personality and establish a consistent visual identity.

For The Mori's Club, the combination of five complementary colors and three typography styles creates a brand that feels feminine, elegant, romantic, modern, and sophisticated.

The color palette establishes the emotional mood, while the typography creates structure, readability, and personality.

> A beautiful brand is not defined by individual design elements, but by how harmoniously they come together to create a lasting impression.
