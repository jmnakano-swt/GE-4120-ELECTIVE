## Ⅰ. THE BRAND STORY

*Beauty is not always loud. Sometimes, it is found in the smallest details.*

</div>

The Mori's Club is a fictional beauty brand created around the idea of effortless elegance. Its identity combines femininity, romance, and sophistication to create a visual experience that feels personal, graceful, and timeless.

The brand takes inspiration from delicate details, vintage luxury, soft femininity, and the quiet beauty of simplicity.

## Ⅴ. DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

</div>

| ELEMENT | DESIGN DIRECTION |
|:---|:---|
| Color | Muted, romantic, and sophisticated |
| Typography | Editorial serif paired with modern sans-serif |
| Layout | Clean spacing with intentional visual hierarchy |
| Personality | Feminine, graceful, and confident |
| Overall feel | Quiet luxury with a romantic touch |

The goal is to create a recognizable identity without making the design feel crowded. Every color, font, and detail should contribute to the same visual story.
